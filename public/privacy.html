Privacy

How GreenLoop handles user data.

This is the minimum privacy baseline for a student-facing commercial product.

What we collect

Name, email, student verification details, listings, messages, service requests, job applications, and support submissions.

Why we collect it

To run the marketplace, verify student users, coordinate logistics, moderate misuse, and resolve support issues.

Who can access it

Authorized GreenLoop administrators and the user directly involved in the relevant transaction or request.

Uploads

User uploads are restricted to common images and PDF files. Unsupported file types are blocked.

Questions

For privacy questions, use the support page and choose the Trust & Safety category.
