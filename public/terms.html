Marketplace conduct

Users must post accurate listings, set realistic pickup details, and avoid counterfeit, illegal, or unsafe goods.

Verification

GreenLoop may restrict features until verification is completed and may revoke access for misuse or false claims.

Services and requests

Reservations, deliveries, donations, and service requests may be reviewed, updated, or cancelled by admins to keep operations workable.

Job postings and applications

Employers and admins are responsible for posting accurate roles. Applicants are responsible for the correctness of submitted information and CV files.

Abuse and enforcement

GreenLoop may remove listings, roles, applications, or accounts that create risk, fraud, harassment, or operational abuse.
