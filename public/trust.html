Trust & Safety

How GreenLoop reduces common marketplace risk.

A commercial site needs visible trust signals, not just backend assumptions.

Student-first verification

Accounts are tied to University of Auckland email domains and verified before access to the full marketplace workflow.

Admin review tools

Admins can review users, listings, service requests, opportunities, and job applications from one control surface.

Upload restrictions

Only supported images and PDF files are accepted for uploads, which reduces obvious abuse and unsafe file handling.

Rate protection

Authentication, uploads, and support flows are protected through tighter validation, route checks, and admin review rather than being treated as open public endpoints.
